We Started With
One Perfect Tee.

DAURA & CO. began in a small Kathmandu studio with a simple frustration: it was strangely hard to find a great white t-shirt. Not a cheap one. Not an expensive one. A great one.

After eleven fabric samples and three factory visits, the perfect tee was born — and so was DAURA & CO. Six years later, we design full collections the same way: slowly, carefully, and only when something is truly worth adding to your wardrobe.

Today we're a team of 24 across Kathmandu and Pokhara, still sampling three times before anything reaches you.

What Guides Us

Our Three Promises

Honest Materials

Natural, traceable fibres wherever possible — organic cotton, European flax linen, responsibly-sourced wool and RWS-certified cashmere.

Fair Production

We work with a small circle of audited partner factories and visit each one in person, at least once a year. No exceptions.

Less, Better

Small runs, no mass overproduction, and designs made to outlast seasons. When something sells out, we only remake it if you ask.
